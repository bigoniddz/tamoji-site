Common questions

My purchase is missing on one device.

Open Tamoji on that device and use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Keep your iPhone and Watch nearby and open Tamoji on both. Each device verifies purchase ownership with Apple; pet synchronization does not grant ownership.

Watch gameplay requires Watch Access or Remove Ads + Watch Access. Cosmetic packs are separate purchases. If an error persists, send the exact message and the product name.

The price currency or App Store account looks wrong.

Tamoji displays prices provided by Apple. Reopen the app and use the retry option shown on the purchase screen, if available. For a sandbox beta, confirm the tester and storefront match your intended test account. Don’t change your personal Apple Account’s country as a first troubleshooting step. Send us the complete alert, including any country it names.

My phone and Watch aren’t showing the same pet.

Keep the paired devices nearby with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled, and open Tamoji on both. Background delivery is managed by the system and may take time. Both apps must use compatible versions.

If both devices already had pets, open Settings → Your shared friend to inspect the selection and recovery options. Don’t delete either app to troubleshoot a missing pet.

Can I recover my pet after deleting the app?

Pet saves and recovery copies are stored locally. Phone–Watch sync is not an account-based cloud backup. Deleting both installations can remove your pet. Contact us before uninstalling an app to fix a save issue.

A bonus video didn’t load or give a reward.

Rewarded bonuses require the video provider’s earned-reward callback. Cancelling a video or a failed load does not grant a bonus. If the error includes Video details, send its diagnostic code and build number, plus whether you were using Wi-Fi or cellular. Don’t include account credentials.

I need help with a refund.

Apple handles App Store payments and refunds. You can review your purchases and request help through Apple’s purchase support. We can help investigate missing access, but we don’t receive your payment-card details or process Apple refunds ourselves.
