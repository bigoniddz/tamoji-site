Tamoji Privacy

Your little friend.
Your information.

Effective 29 October 2026 · Developer: Eren Gevgili

This policy describes the current Tamoji app for iPhone and Apple Watch, including its beta advertising configuration, and this support website.

Pet dataPurchasesAdvertisingSupportWebsiteYour choices

Pet data stays on your devices.

Tamoji stores your pet, care actions, settings, game progress, room selections and remembered lives locally. Your iPhone and its paired Apple Watch can exchange saved pet journals through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. There is no separate Tamoji login or developer-operated account or pet-save server in the current app.

Local saves can include device-specific synchronization identifiers, timestamps, action histories, recovery copies and migration archives. They support saving, paired-device synchronization and recovery; they are not advertising profiles.

These records remain on the device until removed through app or operating-system actions. Paired-device synchronization is not a cloud backup. Deleting both apps can remove the saved pet. Apple-managed backups and other system services are subject to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Purchases are handled by Apple.

Apple processes In-App Purchases and refunds. Tamoji uses StoreKit transaction information, such as product identifiers and verified entitlements, to provide the access features and cosmetic collections you own. The app does not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account password through this purchase flow.

Purchase ownership is checked separately on each device. Pet synchronization does not transfer trusted purchase ownership. Apple’s handling of purchase and account information is described in Apple’s privacy policy.

Advertising on iPhone

The iPhone app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform. The Watch app and widget do not include these advertising SDKs. Current beta builds use Google’s official test advertising units, request non-personalized ads, disable publisher first-party ID, and do not request App Tracking Transparency authorization.

Test ads and non-personalized requests do not mean the advertising services collect no information. Google documents that its iOS Mobile Ads SDK may collect IP addresses (which can indicate general location), device or app identifiers, advertising activity, app interactions, crash logs and performance information for advertising, measurement, analytics and diagnostics.

Google processes information under its policies and the applicable SDK configuration. See Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure, Google’s privacy policy and Google’s advertising information. The app does not send your pet’s saved journal to Google through its advertising integration.

The current test-only advertising setup is not a claim that a production consent flow has been validated. This policy will be reviewed if production advertising or other data-handling features change. When an applicable ad-privacy-options form is required and configured, the app provides Ad privacy choices.

Diagnostics and support

Tamoji records troubleshooting information such as product identifiers, storefront/currency information, purchase error codes and advertising failure details. Advertising diagnostics can include a Google response identifier and app build number. The app does not intentionally log account passwords, payment-card details or full purchase receipts.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond, troubleshoot and follow up on your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information that isn’t needed for the issue. You can contact us to ask about your support correspondence or request its deletion.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no website analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or externally hosted fonts. Its email links open your email application.

GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, when serving the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its hosting and security practices.

Your choices and questions

You can pause pet care, manage notifications through system Settings, and use the app’s available purchase restoration and privacy controls. Local app data can be removed through operating-system app deletion, which may also remove your pet; ask for help before deleting an installation you want to preserve. Information held by Apple or Google is managed through their respective services and policies.

For privacy questions, support requests or questions from a parent or guardian, email support@picklz.app. This policy’s effective date will change when its content is updated.
